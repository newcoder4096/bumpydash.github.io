# BUMPY DASH Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11/2/2016
Effective date: 11/2/2016

BUMPY DASH (the "Game") is developed and operated by gamemaker ("we", "us"). We take your personal information and privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the Game processes while it runs, how that information is used, and what rights you have. Please read it carefully before playing. By starting the Game, you confirm that you have read and agree to this policy.

## 1. Information we collect and use

### 1.1 Information the Game does not collect

The Game has no account system. You do not need to register or log in, and the Game does not access your phone number, name, avatar, nickname, contacts, location, photo library, camera or microphone. The Game has no payment or top-up features.

### 1.2 Information stored only on your device

To keep your progress and preferences, the Game stores the following data on your device using the local storage provided by the mini-game platform:

- Stage progress: unlocked chapters and stages, and the stars earned on each stage;
- Item data: how many items you own, and your progress toward rewarded-video rewards;
- Game settings: whether background music and sound effects are on, and the interface language.

This data stays on your device. We do not upload it to our servers (the Game has no server of its own). If you delete the Game or clear its cache, this data is deleted with it and cannot be recovered.

### 1.3 Device display information

To fit the game screen to different phones, the Game reads display parameters such as screen size, safe area and the position of the platform's menu button in the top corner. These values are used on your device only, to calculate the interface layout. They are neither stored nor uploaded.

### 1.4 Advertising

The Game uses advertising services provided by the mini-game platform, including rewarded video ads, interstitial ads and banner ads:

- Rewarded video: shown only after you actively tap "Watch video". Watching it in full grants an item or unlocks a chapter.
- Interstitial: may appear after you clear a stage, subject to display intervals and frequency limits.
- Banner: may appear at the top of the stage screen.

Ads are served by the mini-game platform and its advertising partners. To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, those partners may collect device information (such as device model, operating system version and device identifiers), network information, and ad impression and click data under their own privacy policies. We do not receive information from the advertising service that identifies you personally. For how that information is handled, please refer to the privacy policies published by the mini-game platform and its advertising partners.

## 2. Sharing, transfer and public disclosure

Apart from the platform advertising service described above, we do not share, transfer or publicly disclose your personal information to any third party. If we are required to provide information by laws, regulations, or a judicial or administrative authority, we will do so in accordance with the law.

## 3. Storage and security

Progress and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not store your personal information on any server. We take reasonable technical measures to protect local data, but please understand that no security measure can be absolutely secure.

## 4. Your rights

- Access and correction: you can change music, sound effects and language at any time in the Game's settings screen.
- Deletion: you can delete all locally stored game data by deleting the Game or clearing its cache.
- Advertising: whether to watch a rewarded video is entirely up to you, and declining does not affect normal play. For settings such as personalized advertising, please adjust them in the mini-game platform or in your device's system settings.

## 5. Protection of minors

The Game is suitable for players of all ages and does not actively collect personal information from minors. Children under 14 should play with the accompaniment and guidance of a guardian. Guardians who have questions about how a child's information is handled may contact us using the details below.

## 6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game's features change or as laws and regulations require. The updated policy will be published in the Game or on the relevant page of the mini-game platform and takes effect on the date of publication. If there are material changes, we will notify you in an appropriate way.
